About Us

Samrakshana Yoga Kendra

Samrakshana Yoga Kendra, nestled in the heart of Raja Annamalaipuram, Chennai, is a premier yoga centre dedicated to holistic well-being for every individual. Led by the passionate and highly skilled instructor Ramya, the centre offers a warm, personalized learning environment where students of all ages and fitness levels are welcomed with care and commitment. With an outstanding rating of 4.9 out of 5, the kendra has become a beloved space for hundreds of students seeking physical flexibility, mental clarity, and overall health transformation.

At Samrakshana Yoga Kendra, every student receives individual attention tailored to their unique body and goals. From prenatal yoga for expecting mothers to flexibility training and breathing techniques for everyday wellness, Ramya brings years of expertise, patience, and genuine passion to each session. The centre also offers flexible online classes to accommodate different time zones, making authentic yoga guidance accessible far beyond Chennai. Open six days a week from early morning to evening, this is your sanctuary to grow, heal, and thrive.
